Technique 01 — Empathy Map

Who Is Marc
Right Now?

Marc
The user
Developer building his own personal finance app — both a daily tool he actually needs and a portfolio piece that demonstrates he can take a product from idea to production.
  • Telegram works, but it's clunky for finance — no way to see, fix, or query what Conchita registered.
  • The app must be as fast or faster to open as Telegram, or he won't use it.
  • It needs to be portfolio-grade — CI/CD, tests, docs, the full stack — not just working code.
  • Conchita's personality needs to be coherent across every screen, not a skin slapped on.
  • Friction when Conchita misclassifies — can't fix it without digging through Telegram history.
  • Satisfaction when logging an expense becomes as natural as taking a photo.
  • Slightly anxious about the analytics side — what if the data is messy from past misclassifications?
  • Pride in the typewriter concept — it's distinctive, it's his.
  • "Entry must be as fast as or faster than Telegram."
  • "I want a drum roller, like an iOS time picker — consistent with the typewriter feel."
  • "Conchita should be patiently waiting for receipts. Never pushy. Never predictive."
  • "The empty state needs personality — Conchita's funny lines, muted so it feels secondary."
  • Can't see what Conchita registered without scrolling through Telegram.
  • Can't correct misclassifications after the fact without manual DB edits.
  • No way to tag expenses to a trip or context — 'Italy holiday' is just noise in the feed.
  • Timeout / failure means re-typing the whole expense from scratch.
  • Gmail invoices arrive but there's no clean flow to review or assign them.
  • Open the app instinctively after every expense — it becomes a habit, not a chore.
  • A journal that's pleasant to browse on the couch: clean, muted, honest.
  • Analytics that let him conclude: "I'm spending too much on subscriptions."
  • A showcase project a recruiter can clone, read, and actually run.
Technique 02 — SCAMPER

Redesigning the Flow,
Letter by Letter

S
Substitute — input method
Replace structured forms with natural language + photo + voice. Conchita interprets; the user only sees what she understood. If she's wrong, one tap opens a confirmation card with defaults already filled — a drum roller for category, a number pad for amount. No form the user has to fill cold.
resolved by user
C
Combine — two flows into one
Context creation + expense log in one breath. Mention "add to Barcelona trip" while logging — Conchita creates the context and assigns the expense simultaneously. No separate context management screen. Also: Gmail invoice review + context assignment combined in a single deep-linked card.
coach idea
A
Adapt — the waiting state
The n8n → Gemini round-trip takes time. Rather than a dead spinner, show rotating personality copy: "Conchita is working…", "Conchita is processing your info…" — keeps the typewriter character alive during the only moment of silence in the app.
user idea
M
Modify — magnify the exit, minify the app
Three magnify/minify candidates explored. The winner: home screen widget as primary log interface. Tap widget → speak or type → push notification confirms what Conchita registered. No need to open the app most days. App becomes the power tool for history, dashboard, context management.
pivotal decision
P
Put to other uses — the existing data
Conchita is currently write-only. The data just sits in PostgreSQL. Repurpose it for flexible self-serve analytics: filter by category + period, trend graphs, monthly breakdown, custom category groups. Requires a new n8n analytics agent: query params → SQL → structured response to app.
architecture impact
E
Eliminate — the origin field
The origen field (telegram / gmail / app) is irrelevant to the user. Never surface it in the UI. Keep it in the DB for backend integrity, but hide it completely from every app screen. Eliminate the noise.
user decision
R
Reverse — who initiates
The Gmail invoice flow is already reversed: Conchita initiates, not the user. She detects invoices, processes and saves to Drive, then pushes a notification to the user. App surfaces this as push notification with deep-linked card: PDF preview, context assignment, delete — all in one place.
user idea
Technique 03 — What If Scenarios

Stress-Testing
the Assumptions

01
What if Conchita misclassifies your supermarket as a restaurant?
Confirmation card with drum roller — wrong category is one scroll away. Analytics correction layer: manually deselect individual entries from a view. Swipe-to-reveal edit on every list item, always.
02
What if the n8n → Gemini round-trip times out completely?
Keep the input content. Surface a retry button immediately — no retyping. Failure state is recoverable, never punishing.
03
What if Marc doesn't open the app for a week?
A reminder notification fires after N days of inactivity. Gentle, not pushy — Conchita is patiently waiting, but she can knock once.
04
What if he goes on a two-week trip to Italy?
Activate "Italy trip" context. Every expense auto-tags while active. A persistent indicator is always visible. On return: deactivate, archive. Next year, reactivate the same context and all history is preserved.
05
What if three Gmail invoices arrive from an unknown sender?
Notification always fires for unknown senders — Conchita is asking for input, not informing. User can add sender to known list or delete. Settings: 2 toggles max, no UX maze.
06
What if Marc wants to understand his heating costs each winter?
Analytics screen: filter by utility category, set a recurring seasonal date range. Trend graph over multiple years reveals the spike pattern. Conclusion-oriented, not just numbers.

Technique 05 — Backcasting

Working
Backwards
from Habit

North Star — Future State
Opening the app instinctively after every expense
Quick photo of a receipt. A sentence about a coffee. Done — without thinking about it. The app has become a reflex, not a decision. The expense journal is a pleasant artifact to browse on the couch.
Late milestone — Habit formed
The journal is worth browsing
Analytics are populated with months of real data. Trends are visible — heating cost spikes in December, subscriptions creeping up, groceries stable. Browsing the journal delivers genuine insight, which rewards continued logging.
Mid milestone — Data trust
The data is trustworthy
Correction flows work. Misclassifications are caught and fixed. Context tagging means that "Italy trip" data is cleanly separated from everyday spend. Consistent data entry is the foundation — good journal requires good data, good data requires habit.
Early milestone — Friction eliminated
Entry beats Telegram
Widget tapped. Expense spoken or typed. Push notification confirms. No app opened, no form filled. The speed bar Telegram set is cleared — so Marc reaches for the app instead.
Root — What must be true on day one
The widget exists and is instant
The home screen widget is the first thing Marc ships. Everything else — analytics, contexts, Gmail cards — is built after the entry habit exists. Habit is the precondition for everything downstream. Future: reminder notification if app unused for N days, a gentle knock from Conchita.
Synthesis — The Climax

What This Session
Actually Decided

Insight 01 — Identity
Conchita's character is one coherent identity
The typewriter aesthetic, drum rollers, muted category colors, rotating loading copy, personality-filled empty states — these are not separate design choices. They are one thing. A recruiter looking at the app should feel a single author's hand throughout. Every screen either reinforces the identity or breaks it. There is no neutral.
Insight 02 — Product Strategy
The widget is the primary product.
The app is the power tool.
This reframes the entire information architecture. The app is not the thing Marc uses every day — the widget is. The app is what Marc opens when something went wrong, when he wants to browse history, when he's doing analytics on a Sunday afternoon. Build the widget first. Ship the habit loop. Then build the rest.
Insight 03 — Product Funnel
The product strategy is a causal chain
Five steps, each one unlocking the next. You cannot skip ahead.
Step 1
Frictionless entry
(widget + instant)
Step 2
Consistent data
(habit + correction)
Step 3
Trustworthy journal
(clean, accurate)
Step 4
Pleasant browsing
(design + context)
Step 5
The habit
(reflex, not decision)
Insight 04 — Architecture
Conchita is request-response — and that's a constraint, not a flaw
n8n → Gemini is synchronous. No streaming, no live parse. The confirmation card appears after a full round-trip. The loading state must do real work — it is the only moment of character the app has while waiting. Use it. Conchita's personality copy in the loading spinner is not decoration; it is load-bearing UX.
Insight 05 — Scope
V1 is two petals, not nine
Log fast. See clearly. That is version one. AI-generated insights, spending predictions, proactive coaching — all deferred. Conchita is patiently waiting for receipts, never proactive, never pushy. The lotus blossom showed the full possibility space so we could confidently choose to ignore most of it.
The Widget

This is where the habit is built or broken.
Ship this first. Everything else is downstream.
